# Failure-State Path for n = 8,803,369

**Date:** 2026-08-28  
**Status:** Exact centered-product analysis of the extremal case in the corrected 10^7 cascade

The integer

\[
\boxed{n=8,803,369}
\]

is the final survivor before `u=107` in the corrected classical-core cascade through `10^7`.

For each gate let

\[
x_u=\frac{n+u}{4},\qquad M_u=n x_u.
\]

The exact centered criterion asks whether `-1` is reachable from bounded signed prime exponents of `M_u` modulo u.

Two distinct failure mechanisms occur:

1. **subgroup-pure failure:** the subgroup generated by the prime-factor residues does not contain `-1`;
2. **capacity failure:** the generated subgroup contains `-1`, but the bounded exponent box does not reach it.

## Interpretation

The early gates are not failing because of a small random miss inside a large divisor set.  Through `u=39`, every failure is explained by a proper generated subgroup omitting `-1`.

At `u=43` a new phenomenon begins: the prime-factor residues generate a subgroup containing `-1`, but the bounded valuation capacities are too small to reach it.  At some later prime gates the generated subgroup is the entire unit group and the method still fails:

- `u=59`: full group of size 58 generated, but only 27 residues are capacity-reachable;
- `u=67`: full group of size 66 generated, but only 23 residues are reachable;
- `u=79`: full group of size 78 generated, but only 39 residues are reachable;
- `u=83`: full group of size 82 generated, but only 27 residues are reachable;
- `u=103`: full group of size 102 generated, but only 49 residues are reachable.

Finally at `u=107`, the bounded centered set covers all 106 units and reaches `-1`.

## Consequence for proof strategy

A character/subgroup argument alone cannot prove the full cascade: it explains the dominant early failures but not the late capacity exceptions.  A complete proof needs two layers:

1. rule out persistent proper-subgroup purity across shifts;
2. when the generated subgroup contains `-1`, prove sufficient bounded product-set growth to overcome the finite capacity exceptions.

This extremal example should remain a regression test for every proposed general theorem.
